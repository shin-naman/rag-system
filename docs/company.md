# Nimbus Coffee Roasters — Company Overview

## Founding and History

Nimbus Coffee Roasters was founded in 2014 by Mara Delacroix and Toby Fenn in
Portland, Oregon. The two met while working at a small café in the Alberta Arts
District and bonded over a shared frustration: they felt that most local coffee
was either burnt-tasting dark roast or inconsistent from cup to cup. They pooled
their savings, bought a used cart, and began selling pour-over coffee on weekends.

The company began as that single cart in the Alberta Arts District in 2014 and
opened its first permanent café in March 2016 on Northeast Killingsworth Street.
The early café had only eight seats and a single espresso machine, but it quickly
developed a loyal following among neighborhood regulars and nearby office workers.

As of 2023, Nimbus operates seven café locations across Oregon and Washington.
Five of the cafés are in the Portland metro area, one is in Salem, and one is in
Vancouver, Washington. All coffee is roasted at a central facility in Southeast
Portland, which the company moved into in 2019 after outgrowing the back room of
the original café.

## Mission and Values

Nimbus describes its mission as "making genuinely good coffee approachable without
being precious about it." The founders have said in interviews that they want
customers to feel welcome whether they know nothing about coffee or are seasoned
enthusiasts. Staff are trained to explain the differences between roasts in plain
language rather than industry jargon.

## Leadership

Mara Delacroix serves as the head of roasting and green-coffee buying. Toby Fenn
oversees café operations and staff training. The company has deliberately stayed
independent and has not taken outside investment, which the founders say lets them
prioritize sourcing relationships over rapid expansion.
