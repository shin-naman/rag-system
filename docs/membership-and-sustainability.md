# Membership, Sustainability, and Store Info

## Cloud Club Membership

The Cloud Club loyalty program costs $9 per month. Members receive one free
12-ounce bag of any house blend each month, plus 10 percent off all in-store
drinks. Membership can be cancelled at any time with no penalty, and there is no
annual plan — the program is strictly month to month. Members also get early
access to seasonal single-origin releases, typically a full day before they are
announced to the general newsletter list.

To sign up, customers create an account at the counter or through the website.
Billing occurs on the same day each month. If a member forgets to redeem their
free bag in a given month, it does not roll over to the next month.

## Sustainability

All Nimbus cafés have used compostable cups and lids since January 2022. Before
that, the company used standard paper cups with plastic lids, and switching over
required finding a supplier whose compostable lids would not warp under hot
liquid. The roasting facility runs on 100 percent renewable electricity through a
local utility program.

The company has a stated goal of becoming carbon neutral by 2030. Its published
plan focuses on three areas: reducing shipping emissions by consolidating green-
coffee imports into fewer, larger shipments; investing in more efficient roasting
equipment; and purchasing verified carbon offsets for emissions it cannot yet
eliminate. Nimbus reports progress against this goal in the same annual report
that covers sourcing.

## Hours and Contact

Cafés are open daily from 6:30 AM to 7:00 PM, except the downtown Portland
location, which closes at 9:00 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. The main customer
support line is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time.
Questions about wholesale orders should be directed to the wholesale team by
email rather than the main support line.
